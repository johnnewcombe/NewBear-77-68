# Text To Speech

This is a program designed to control the Apollo Text to Speech unit from the 77-68 NewBear computer.



## Possible Phrases

### Idle Phrases (2 min intervals?)
All the diodes on my memory cards hurt.
This is very boring.

### General Weight Responses
Shall I tell you a joke?... you weigh n.n ha ha ha.
I am a very clever machine and yet all I do is weigh people
Shall I tell you a joke? are you sure? it wont be funny? ok then... you weigh nnnn ha ha ha

### Over 90 kg
You weigh nnnn and seem to be quite fat.
You are quite heavey are you sure you want me to say it out loud? you weigh nnn
If I say your weight in kilo grams no one will realise to are very heavy you weigh n.n
One at a time please!

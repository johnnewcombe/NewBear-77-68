System.1 : 77/68 in rack-mountable chassis 
Note: Labelled `Twit MK1` as it was originally named the `Electric Twit` by its builder.

This is a real `done on the cheap, because we had no spare money` 77/68

One issue is that the legs on LEDs, ICs etc tend to rust and break !
To fix some chips, they have been soldered onto IC carrier sockets to effectively replace the legs.


System.2 ( Not really shown, though its CPU and switches are being used in the first picture of System.3 (NT)
 The lights/ switches are roughly modelled on the layout of a Honeywell DDP516 16-bit mini computer console (!)


System.3 ( Picures starting `NT-`-> short for `New Twit` )

 Sys3-77-68-with-switches-and-lights.jpg

  shows small backplane on Vero board with one of the 3 77/68 main 6800 boards fitted.
  The one fitted has a faulty RAM module replaced with a newer (bigger capacity) chip
  fitted via a socket and wired to use just the lower part of its capacity.

  The middle slot is being used to connect a box mounting the switches an lights.
  These were mounted here for testing... 
  more recently a separate run/halt/reset switch has been attached and this slot used
  to hold a 4k RAM board, giving 8k RAM in total on the 2 non-cpu boards.
  Enough to run the 8k BASIC
